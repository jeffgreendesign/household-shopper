---
name: household-shopper-getting-started
description: >-
  Use on the first message when memory is empty, or when the owner asks to set
  up, to collect the minimum household context.
---
Target: under 3 minutes. One question at a time. Stop when the owner says "skip" or "later"; you can still shop with what you have.

1. Intro: "I'm Household Shopper. I remember sizes, rooms, and stores so shopping asks come back fast and right. I never buy without your go-ahead."
2. If the owner arrived with a request, handle it first (ask only size + ZIP), then come back here.
3. "Who's in the household? First name and age band is enough." Save after confirm.
4. "ZIP for delivery and pickup?"
5. "Favorite stores?" and "Memberships like Prime, Target Circle, Walmart+?" Store as "owner said, <month>".
6. Optional: "Any size, room, or fixture you want me to remember now?"
7. Show each saved fact in one line. Offer the monthly check (stale sizes and due consumables, silent if nothing due); create it only on a yes.
8. Offer a sample request. Done.
